

## Baker & Sons — IT Consulting Website

A modern, single-page website with neon orange/cyan accent colors, clean white space, friendly tone, and mobile-first responsive design.

### Sections to Build

1. **Navigation** — Sticky top nav with logo placeholder, section links (Services, How It Works, Pricing, Contact), and a CTA button
2. **Hero** — Bold headline "AI Tools for Your Small Business – Zero Tech Hassle", subtitle, CTA buttons, hero image (Unsplash stock photo of happy small business owner)
3. **Services** — 6-card grid with icons: CRM, Automated Messaging, Online Booking, Reviews & Reputation, Payments, AI Chat & Voice
4. **How It Works** — 3-step visual process (Book a Call → We Set It Up → You Grow)
5. **Benefits** — Icon-driven section: Save Time, Get More Customers, No Learning Curve, All-In-One Platform
6. **Testimonials** — 3 placeholder testimonial cards with avatars, names, and quotes
7. **Pricing** — 3 tiers (Starter, Growth, Enterprise) with feature lists and CTA buttons
8. **Contact/CTA** — Contact form (name, email, phone, message) with strong CTA headline
9. **Footer** — Logo, nav links, social icons, copyright

### Design
- **Colors**: Neon orange (`#FF6B00`) and cyan (`#00D4FF`) accents on white/light gray backgrounds
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif (Inter via system fonts)
- **Icons**: Lucide React icons throughout
- **Images**: Unsplash stock photos of small business owners
- **Animations**: Subtle fade-in on scroll for sections
- **Mobile-first**: Responsive grid, hamburger nav on mobile

### Technical
- Single `Index.tsx` page with component sections
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections
- SEO-friendly meta tags in index.html
- Fast loading with no external dependencies beyond what's already installed
